VAT & EU orders

All displayed prices are in GBP and include UK VAT at 20%. The checkout breaks out the VAT portion. Where we ship DDP, duties and VAT are handled before dispatch so there is nothing extra to pay on delivery.

Free 28-day UK returns

Return unworn pieces with tags attached within 28 days. Contact the atelier with your order number and we will email a prepaid Royal Mail label. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 working days of the return reaching us.

How to return

  1. Email hello@linenandloom.co.uk or call +44 20 7946 0958 with your order number.
  2. Place the piece in its original linen dust bag or a plain recyclable parcel.
  3. Attach the prepaid label and drop it at any Royal Mail parcel point.
  4. We email you when it arrives and when the refund is released.

Exchanges

Need a different UK size? Return the original and place a new order, or ask the atelier to hold the replacement size for 48 hours while your return travels.
