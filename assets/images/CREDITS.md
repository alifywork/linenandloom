# Photography credits

All photography is stored locally in this folder and is served from the site itself — there are no remote image URLs. The imagery is Pexels-derived editorial/stock photography, lightly colour-graded to the LINEN & LOOM palette. `tools/prepare_images.py` documents the raw filename used for each local asset and the normalisation applied. Product names, prices, reviews, atelier copy and store details are fictional storefront content created for this brief.
