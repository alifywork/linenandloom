Our cloth

From flax field to finished seam

We choose cloth before we sketch a silhouette. It is a slower way to work, but it is the only way we know how to make a wardrobe that lasts.

01

Source

Belgian flax, organic cotton and mulberry silk from mills we visit. Every roll is checked for hand-feel, weight and how it will age.

02

Cut

Patterns are drafted at 48 Redchurch Street, graded to UK 6–18 and cut in small batches so nothing is over-produced.

03

Finish

Seams are pressed, buttons are sewn by hand and each piece is checked against the original sample before it leaves the atelier.
